# Short months and whole years

Next, two parts of "Which dates a rule produces" from my first message change. Each replaces what that message said.

## Interface changes

No names or signatures change. `parse_rule` accepts larger `BYDAY` ordinals in one case (below). The rest is behaviour. Descriptions from `describe` stay as they are.

## Short months

Old: with neither `BYMONTHDAY` nor `BYDAY`, the month rule takes the start's day of the month and skips months that do not have it.

New: it takes the start's day of the month, or the last day of the month when the month is shorter. So `FREQ=MONTHLY` from 2026-01-31 gives 2026-01-31, 2026-02-28, 2026-03-31, 2026-04-30, and `FREQ=YEARLY` from 2024-02-29 gives 2024-02-29, 2025-02-28, 2026-02-28, 2027-02-28, 2028-02-29. Days listed in `BYMONTHDAY` that a month does not have are still skipped, as before.

## Yearly rules without BYMONTH

Old: `FREQ=YEARLY` with an empty `BYMONTH` always works in the start's month.

New: that stays so when neither `BYMONTHDAY` nor `BYDAY` is given. When `BYMONTH` is empty and at least one of them is given, the candidates of an active year come from the whole year:

- `BYMONTHDAY` only: the listed days in every month of the year (a month without such a day is skipped).
- `BYDAY` only: a listed weekday without an ordinal gives every such weekday of the year. With ordinal n it gives the n-th such weekday of the year counted from January 1, or for n < 0 counted back from December 31. One that the year does not have is skipped.
- Both: only the days that match both lists.

With `FREQ=YEARLY` and an empty `BYMONTH` (whether or not `BYMONTHDAY` is given), `BYDAY` ordinals may be 1 to 53 or -53 to -1. With `FREQ=MONTHLY`, or `FREQ=YEARLY` with `BYMONTH`, the range stays 1 to 5 and -5 to -1, and a larger ordinal raises `RuleError`.

Examples from 2026-01-01: `FREQ=YEARLY;BYDAY=-1FR;COUNT=2` gives 2026-12-25 and 2027-12-31. `FREQ=YEARLY;BYDAY=20MO;COUNT=1` gives 2026-05-18. From 2026-03-15, `FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTHDAY=1;COUNT=3` gives 2026-04-01, 2026-05-01 and 2026-06-01.
